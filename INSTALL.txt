Laser Sentry Cooldown v1.1
Supports Helldivers 2 Steam build 25480438 / EXE 1.8.46015.0.

Install Bingus Shared Loader v18 or newer and import this ZIP into Arsenal or
HD2MM. Enable and deploy both, then restart Helldivers 2. Keep the shared
loader as the winning Wwise startup replacement.

What it changes, for the A/LAS-98 Laser Sentry only:
- At max heat it overheats and stops firing as usual, but it does not
  explode: it cools at its normal rate (about 50 s from max heat on a normal
  planet), its turret powers up again and it fires again.
- As with other lasers that recover, it also cools a little while its beam
  powers up or winds down (up to about 3.5 of 250 heat per burst).
No options and no new numbers: every value is the sentry's own game data.
Heat build-up while firing, idle cooling, damage and targeting are unchanged.

Log: %LOCALAPPDATA%/CowboyBingus/Helldivers2/Logs/LaserSentryCooldown.log
